Cookies Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how boilers.repair ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on boilers.repair (the "Site"), in line with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, and to provide information to the website owner. Similar technologies (such as tracking pixels and local storage) work in comparable ways and are covered by this policy.

2. Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the Site to function properly (for example, remembering your cookie consent choices) and cannot be switched off. As they are essential, they do not require your consent under PECR.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics (or similar analytics tools) to understand how visitors use the Site — for example, which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and how they arrived at the Site. This helps us improve the Site's content and performance. These cookies collect information in an anonymous or pseudonymous form where possible.

Advertising and retargeting cookies

We use advertising and retargeting cookies, including from Google Ads and Meta (Facebook) Pixel, to:

  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns; and
  • Show you relevant adverts for our services when you visit other websites or platforms (this is sometimes called "retargeting" or "remarketing").

These cookies track your browsing activity across the Site and may be linked with your activity on other sites to build a profile of your interests for advertising purposes.

4. Your consent choices

Because analytics and advertising cookies are not strictly necessary, UK law requires us to ask for your consent before setting them. When you first visit the Site, you should see a cookie banner allowing you to:

  • Accept all cookies (necessary, analytics, and advertising);
  • Reject non-essential cookies (only strictly necessary cookies will be set); or
  • Manage preferences, choosing which categories to allow.

You can change your preferences at any time by [reopening the cookie settings tool / clearing your cookies via your browser settings — update this to match whatever cookie-consent tool you use, e.g. a "Cookie Settings" link in your footer].

5. Managing cookies through your browser

In addition to our cookie banner, you can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them individually;
  • Block third-party cookies;
  • Block cookies from particular sites; or
  • Block all cookies.

Note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of this and other websites. Guidance for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

You can also opt out of interest-based advertising directly with some providers:

  • Meta: Meta Ad Preferences
  • Industry-wide opt-out: Your Online Choices (EU/UK)
